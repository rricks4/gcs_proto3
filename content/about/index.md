Global Commerce and Services, LLC (GCS) is an SBA Certified HUBZone and Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business specializing in Information Technology and founded in 2000.

GCS has experience in Information Technology, including both management and technical expertise, and has provided services including Systems Engineering, Cybersecurity, Local Area Network/Wide Area Network (LAN/WAN) Development and Support, and Program/Project Management. Our clients include Department of Agriculture, Department of Navy, U. S. Airforce, U. S. Coast Guard, Department of Army, U.S. Peace Corps, Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA), and The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

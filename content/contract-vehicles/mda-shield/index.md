The MDA SHIELD (Scalable Homeland Innovative Enterprise Layered Defense) is the core framework for U.S. homeland missile defense, integrating sensors, interceptors, command and control systems, and supporting infrastructure into a coordinated, layered defense posture. It is not a single platform or program but a system-of-systems construct that governs how missile defense capabilities are developed, deployed, upgraded, and synchronized across multiple domains
